How it works

From logo to bag in three steps

  1. Choose the bag

    Pick a bag type, a standard size, the fabric colour and the handles.

  2. Place your design

    Add your logo, text and a QR code. The front and the back each get their own design.

  3. Send it to us

    Save the 3D image and send it with the quantity you need. The minimum order depends on the size.

Material

Soft, light and made to be used again

Our bags are spunbond polypropylene: a soft, paper-like fabric that is light, reusable and recyclable.

Fabric
Non-woven polypropylene with an embossed dot texture
Seams
Joined by ultrasonic welding, with no thread
Handles
Non-woven straps, woven webbing, die-cut, T-shirt cut or drawstring cord, depending on the bag
Print
One ink colour or full colour, on one side or both
