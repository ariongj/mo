A closer look at what your bag is made of, and how to make it last.

THE MATERIAL

What is it made of?

Our non-woven bags are made entirely from polypropylene (PP). Every component, including the fabric, handles and stitching, is made from the same material, creating a strong, lightweight and durable product.

We use water-based printing inks to support responsible manufacturing.

AFTER REUSE

Can it be recycled?

Our bags are made from recyclable polypropylene. Using one material throughout the bag helps simplify material recovery. Actual recycling depends on local collection and processing facilities; check whether your local provider accepts non-woven PP bags.

EVERYDAY CARE

How should I care for it?

Simply wipe clean or machine wash on a gentle, low-temperature cycle. Air dry and avoid high heat to preserve the bag’s quality and durability. Reuse it while it remains suitable for the items you carry.
